# PhD in Agriculture in Canada — Guide for a Bangladeshi Applicant

## Visa outlook (good news)

As of **January 2026**, master's and PhD students at public Canadian universities are:

- **Exempt from the international student cap**
- **Exempt from the PAL/TAL (Provincial Attestation Letter) requirement**
- Eligible for a **14-day processing target** for doctoral applicants applying from outside Canada

So despite the headlines about Canada cutting international student numbers, the PhD route is materially easier than the undergraduate or college route right now.

---

## The single most important thing to understand

In Canada, agriculture PhD admission is **supervisor-driven**, not department-driven. A professor with grant money decides to take a student, and the admissions office then processes the paperwork.

Applying to a "good university" without a supervisor who has already agreed to fund you usually results in rejection, or an offer with no funding attached.

**The real strategy:**

1. Identify 20–30 professors whose research matches his work.
2. Email each one individually with a short, tailored message — CV, publication list, and a specific reference to one or two of their papers.
3. See who responds and has funding available.

The university name matters far less than which professor says yes.

## Practical points for his situation

**Government service is an advantage.** Supervisors value applied field experience, and it strengthens the "ties to home country" element of the visa application. He should check whether his ministry allows **study leave or deputation**, since that affects his timing.

**Publications matter more than GPA.** Two or three first-author peer-reviewed papers will get replies that a strong transcript alone will not.

**Tests.** IELTS 6.5–7.0 is the typical requirement. GRE is rarely needed for agriculture programs now.

**Funding.** Usually **CAD 22,000–30,000 per year** from a supervisor's grant plus TA work. This covers tuition and modest living costs, but is tight for supporting a family.

**Timing.** Application deadlines for the September intake generally fall between **December and February**. Contacting supervisors now positions him well for a **September 2027** start.

---

## Suggested next steps

1. Write a clean 2-page academic CV with a publications section.
2. Draft a one-page research statement tied to his current government work.
3. Build a spreadsheet of target supervisors: name, university, research area, recent paper, contact date, response.
4. Send 5–10 emails per week rather than a mass mailing — tailored emails get replies, generic ones do not.
5. Book IELTS early so scores are ready before deadlines.
